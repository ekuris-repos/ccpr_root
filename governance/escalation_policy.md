# Escalation Policy

## Purpose
Define clear escalation procedures for prompt library issues, disputes, and decision-making processes to ensure timely resolution and appropriate stakeholder involvement.

## Scope
This policy covers all escalation scenarios including:
- Prompt quality disputes
- Compliance violations
- Technical issues
- Policy disagreements
- Resource conflicts
- Security incidents

## Escalation Principles

### Timeliness
- Clear response time requirements
- Automatic escalation triggers
- Time-based escalation paths
- Urgency-based prioritization

### Accountability
- Clear ownership at each level
- Decision-making authority
- Responsibility assignments
- Follow-up requirements

### Transparency
- Open communication channels
- Status visibility
- Decision documentation
- Stakeholder notifications

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Team Resolution
**Responsible Party**: Immediate team/supervisor
**Timeline**: 24 hours
**Authority**: Operational decisions, minor policy clarifications
**Escalation Trigger**: Unable to resolve within timeline

**Typical Issues**:
- Prompt template questions
- Minor quality concerns
- Usage guideline clarifications
- Standard categorization disputes

### Level 2: Departmental Management
**Responsible Party**: Department manager/director
**Timeline**: 72 hours
**Authority**: Departmental policy decisions, resource allocation
**Escalation Trigger**: Cross-team impact, policy interpretation needed

**Typical Issues**:
- Resource allocation disputes
- Inter-team coordination problems
- Policy interpretation questions
- Quality standard disagreements

### Level 3: Senior Management
**Responsible Party**: VP/Senior Director level
**Timeline**: 1 week
**Authority**: Strategic decisions, policy changes
**Escalation Trigger**: Significant business impact, policy conflicts

**Typical Issues**:
- Major policy violations
- Strategic direction conflicts
- Significant resource requests
- Cross-departmental disputes

### Level 4: Executive Leadership
**Responsible Party**: C-level executives
**Timeline**: 2 weeks
**Authority**: Strategic direction, major policy changes
**Escalation Trigger**: Company-wide impact, legal implications

**Typical Issues**:
- Legal/compliance concerns
- Major security incidents
- Strategic pivots
- External stakeholder issues

## Issue Categories and Response

### High Priority Issues
**Response Time**: Immediate (within 1 hour)
**Examples**:
- Security vulnerabilities
- Compliance violations
- System outages
- Data breaches

### Medium Priority Issues
**Response Time**: Same business day
**Examples**:
- Quality standard disputes
- Process improvement suggestions
- Resource access problems
- Training needs

### Low Priority Issues
**Response Time**: Within 3 business days
**Examples**:
- Enhancement requests
- Documentation updates
- Non-critical clarifications
- General feedback

## Escalation Procedures

### Step 1: Initial Assessment
1. **Issue Documentation**
   - Detailed problem description
   - Impact assessment
   - Stakeholders involved
   - Attempted resolutions

2. **Classification**
   - Priority level assignment
   - Category identification
   - Urgency determination
   - Resource requirements

### Step 2: First-Level Response
1. **Team Leader Review**
   - Problem analysis
   - Available resources assessment
   - Initial resolution attempt
   - Timeline establishment

2. **Resolution Attempt**
   - Direct problem solving
   - Stakeholder communication
   - Progress monitoring
   - Documentation updates

### Step 3: Escalation Decision
1. **Escalation Criteria Check**
   - Timeline exceeded
   - Authority limitations
   - Resource constraints
   - Stakeholder disputes

2. **Escalation Preparation**
   - Comprehensive documentation
   - Stakeholder notification
   - Handoff preparation
   - Status communication

### Step 4: Higher-Level Review
1. **Management Assessment**
   - Issue review and analysis
   - Decision-making process
   - Resource allocation
   - Action planning

2. **Resolution Implementation**
   - Decision communication
   - Action assignment
   - Timeline establishment
   - Progress monitoring

## Communication Requirements

### Escalation Notifications
- **Immediate**: Affected stakeholders
- **Within 2 hours**: Management chain
- **Daily**: Status updates
- **Upon resolution**: All stakeholders

### Documentation Standards
- **Issue Tracking System**: All escalations logged
- **Resolution Documentation**: Detailed records
- **Decision Rationale**: Clear explanations
- **Lessons Learned**: Process improvements

### Communication Channels
- **Urgent Issues**: Phone/instant messaging
- **Standard Issues**: Email/ticketing system
- **Status Updates**: Dashboard/reports
- **Resolutions**: Formal notifications

## Special Escalation Scenarios

### Security Incidents
1. **Immediate Notification**: Security team
2. **Executive Briefing**: Within 1 hour
3. **Legal Review**: If required
4. **External Notifications**: As needed

### Compliance Violations
1. **Compliance Officer**: Immediate notification
2. **Legal Review**: Risk assessment
3. **Remediation Plan**: Within 24 hours
4. **Regulatory Reporting**: As required

### System Outages
1. **Technical Team**: Immediate response
2. **Business Impact**: Assessment and communication
3. **Stakeholder Updates**: Regular intervals
4. **Post-Incident Review**: Mandatory
