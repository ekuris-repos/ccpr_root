# FERPA Compliance Framework

## Overview
Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act compliance for educational institutions handling student records.

> **Implementation Note**: This FERPA compliance framework is a specialized example for educational institutions. Only implement if your organization is subject to FERPA requirements. Adapt the complexity to match your implementation tier:
> - **Tier 1 (Basic)**: Simple guidelines to avoid student information in prompts
> - **Tier 2 (Intermediate)**: FERPA-aware prompt review process
> - **Tier 3 (Advanced)**: Formal FERPA compliance verification in prompt lifecycle
> - **Tier 4 (Expert)**: Comprehensive FERPA compliance with automated detection and reporting

## Scope of Coverage
- Educational institutions receiving federal funding
- Student educational records
- Directory information
- Disciplinary records
- Financial aid records

## Educational Records Definition
Records directly related to a student maintained by educational institution:
- Academic transcripts
- Grade reports
- Disciplinary files
- Health records (if maintained by school)
- Financial aid documents
- Special education records

## Excluded Records
- Personal notes of instructional staff
- Law enforcement unit records
- Medical treatment records
- Employment records
- Alumni records (post-graduation)

## Student Rights

### Access Rights
- Right to inspect and review educational records
- 45-day response time requirement
- Reasonable access provisions
- Copy request accommodations

### Amendment Rights
- Right to request record corrections
- Formal hearing process for disputes
- Right to add explanatory statements
- Notification of amendment decisions

### Disclosure Control
- Prior written consent requirement
- Limited exceptions for disclosure
- Annual notification requirements
- Opt-out provisions for directory information

## Permitted Disclosures (Without Consent)

### Educational Officials
- Legitimate educational interest
- School employees and contractors
- Officials at other schools (student transfers)
- Audit and evaluation purposes

### Legal Compliance
- Court orders and subpoenas
- Law enforcement (emergency situations)
- Compliance with judicial orders
- Child protective services

### Directory Information
- Student name
- Address and phone number
- Email address
- Date and place of birth
- Participation in activities
- Awards and honors

## Implementation Requirements

### Policy Development
- Written FERPA policies
- Annual notification procedures
- Consent form templates
- Record retention schedules

### Staff Training
- FERPA awareness training
- Proper handling procedures
- Disclosure authorization
- Privacy protection measures

### Record Management
- Secure storage systems
- Access control mechanisms
- Audit trail maintenance
- Retention and disposal

### Technology Safeguards
- Authentication systems
- Encryption requirements
- Network security measures
- Database access controls

## Compliance Procedures

### Annual Notification
- Student rights explanation
- Policy distribution
- Complaint procedures
- Contact information

### Consent Management
- Written consent requirements
- Specific disclosure authorization
- Record of consent maintenance
- Consent withdrawal procedures

### Access Requests
- Identity verification
- Record preparation
- Inspection scheduling
- Copy provision

### Amendment Process
- Request evaluation
- Hearing procedures
- Decision documentation
- Appeal processes

## Penalties and Enforcement
- Federal funding withdrawal
- Compliance investigations
- Corrective action requirements
- Ongoing monitoring obligations

## Best Practices
- Regular policy updates
- Comprehensive staff training
- Robust technology safeguards
- Proactive compliance monitoring
- Clear communication procedures
