# Compliance Monitoring

## Purpose
Establish systematic monitoring and auditing processes for regulatory compliance.

## Framework
Continuous monitoring approach for maintaining compliance across all regulated activities.

## Monitoring Components

### Automated Monitoring
- Real-time compliance checking
- Alert systems for violations
- Automated reporting generation
- Threshold-based notifications

### Manual Reviews
- Periodic compliance audits
- Risk assessments
- Policy adherence verification
- Training effectiveness evaluation

### Documentation Requirements
- Monitoring logs
- Incident reports
- Remediation tracking
- Performance metrics

## Monitoring Matrix

| Regulation | Frequency | Method | Responsibility | Escalation |
|------------|-----------|---------|----------------|------------|
| GDPR | Daily | Automated | DPO | Legal Team |
| HIPAA | Real-time | System | Compliance Officer | Executive |
| SOC2 | Monthly | Manual | Audit Team | Board |
| PCI-DSS | Weekly | Mixed | Security Team | CTO |

## Key Performance Indicators
- Compliance score percentage
- Time to remediation
- Number of violations
- Training completion rates
- Audit findings resolution

## Escalation Procedures
1. Detection
2. Assessment
3. Notification
4. Investigation
5. Remediation
6. Verification
7. Documentation
