# GDPR Compliance Framework

## Overview
General Data Protection Regulation compliance requirements and implementation guidelines.

## Key Principles
1. **Lawfulness, fairness and transparency**
2. **Purpose limitation**
3. **Data minimization**
4. **Accuracy**
5. **Storage limitation**
6. **Integrity and confidentiality**
7. **Accountability**

## Data Subject Rights
- Right to information
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object
- Rights related to automated decision making

## Legal Basis for Processing
- Consent
- Contract
- Legal obligation
- Vital interests
- Public task
- Legitimate interests

## Implementation Requirements

### Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
Required when processing is likely to result in high risk to individuals:
- Systematic and extensive evaluation
- Large scale processing of special categories
- Systematic monitoring of public areas
- New technologies

### Privacy by Design
- Proactive measures
- Privacy as the default setting
- Full functionality protection
- End-to-end security
- Visibility and transparency
- Respect for user privacy

### Data Breach Response
**Timeline**: 72 hours to report to supervisory authority
**Requirements**:
- Nature of breach
- Categories and number of data subjects
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed

## Compliance Checklist
- [ ] Data mapping and inventory
- [ ] Privacy notices updated
- [ ] Consent mechanisms implemented
- [ ] Data subject request procedures
- [ ] Staff training completed
- [ ] Technical safeguards in place
- [ ] Vendor agreements updated
- [ ] Incident response plan
- [ ] Regular compliance audits

## Documentation Requirements
- Records of processing activities
- Data protection impact assessments
- Consent records
- Breach notifications
- Training records
- Audit reports

## Penalties
- Administrative fines up to €20 million or 4% of annual global turnover
- Compensation claims from individuals
- Reputational damage
