# Generate Professional Email Response

Create a polite, professional email response based on the situation described.

**Input situation:** [Describe the email situation or request]

**Generate a professional response that:**
- Addresses the main points
- Uses appropriate tone
- Includes next steps if needed

## Example

**Input:** "Customer is asking for a refund on a product they bought 6 months ago, outside our normal 30-day policy"

**Output:**
```
Subject: Re: Refund Request

Dear [Customer Name],

Thank you for reaching out regarding your recent refund request.

While our standard return policy is 30 days, I understand your situation and want to help find a solution. Let me review your account and discuss options with my manager.

I'll get back to you within 24 hours with next steps.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```
