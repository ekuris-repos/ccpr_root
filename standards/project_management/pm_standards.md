# Project Management Standards

## Purpose
Establish comprehensive project management standards to ensure consistent, effective project delivery across the organization.

## Project Management Framework

### Project Lifecycle
- **Initiation**: Project charter and stakeholder identification
- **Planning**: Scope, schedule, budget, and risk planning
- **Execution**: Work performance and team management
- **Monitoring**: Progress tracking and control
- **Closure**: Project completion and lessons learned

### Methodology Standards
- **Waterfall**: Sequential project phases
- **Agile**: Iterative and incremental development
- **Hybrid**: Combined traditional and agile approaches
- **Lean**: Value-focused, waste elimination
- **DevOps**: Development and operations integration

## Project Initiation Standards

### Project Charter
- **Project Purpose**: Business justification
- **Objectives**: SMART goals and success criteria
- **Scope**: High-level deliverables and boundaries
- **Stakeholders**: Key participants and roles
- **Assumptions**: Project assumptions and constraints

### Stakeholder Management
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Influence and interest assessment
- **Communication Plan**: Stakeholder engagement strategy
- **RACI Matrix**: Responsibility assignment
- **Stakeholder Register**: Contact and role information
- **Engagement Activities**: Regular communication touchpoints

## Planning Standards

### Scope Management
- **Work Breakdown Structure**: Hierarchical task decomposition
- **Scope Statement**: Detailed scope description
- **Requirements Management**: Requirement gathering and tracking
- **Change Control**: Scope change procedures
- **Scope Verification**: Deliverable acceptance criteria

### Schedule Management
- **Activity Definition**: Detailed task identification
- **Activity Sequencing**: Task dependencies
- **Duration Estimation**: Time estimation techniques
- **Schedule Development**: Critical path analysis
- **Schedule Control**: Progress monitoring and updates

### Budget Management
- **Cost Estimation**: Bottom-up and top-down estimates
- **Budget Development**: Resource and cost allocation
- **Cost Control**: Expense tracking and management
- **Earned Value**: Progress and performance measurement
- **Financial Reporting**: Regular budget status updates

## Risk Management Standards

### Risk Assessment
- **Risk Identification**: Systematic risk discovery
- **Risk Analysis**: Probability and impact assessment
- **Risk Prioritization**: Risk ranking and scoring
- **Risk Register**: Centralized risk documentation
- **Risk Reviews**: Regular risk assessment updates

### Risk Response
- **Risk Avoidance**: Eliminate risk causes
- **Risk Mitigation**: Reduce probability or impact
- **Risk Transfer**: Shift risk to third parties
- **Risk Acceptance**: Acknowledge and monitor
- **Contingency Planning**: Response preparation

## Quality Management Standards

### Quality Planning
- **Quality Standards**: Project quality requirements
- **Quality Metrics**: Measurable quality indicators
- **Quality Assurance**: Process compliance verification
- **Quality Control**: Deliverable inspection
- **Continuous Improvement**: Process enhancement

### Quality Processes
- **Reviews and Inspections**: Regular quality checkpoints
- **Testing Procedures**: Verification and validation
- **Documentation Standards**: Consistent documentation
- **Change Management**: Quality impact assessment
- **Lessons Learned**: Knowledge capture and sharing

## Communication Standards

### Communication Planning
- **Communication Requirements**: Stakeholder information needs
- **Communication Methods**: Channels and frequency
- **Communication Matrix**: Who, what, when, how
- **Meeting Standards**: Regular meeting protocols
- **Reporting Standards**: Status and progress reports

### Documentation Standards
- **Project Documentation**: Standardized templates
- **Version Control**: Document management
- **Document Repository**: Centralized storage
- **Access Controls**: Information security
- **Retention Policies**: Document lifecycle

## Resource Management Standards

### Human Resource Management
- **Resource Planning**: Skill and capacity requirements
- **Team Acquisition**: Resource sourcing and allocation
- **Team Development**: Training and skill building
- **Performance Management**: Individual and team performance
- **Resource Optimization**: Efficient resource utilization

### Procurement Management
- **Vendor Selection**: Supplier evaluation and selection
- **Contract Management**: Agreement monitoring
- **Vendor Performance**: Supplier assessment
- **Contract Closure**: Final deliverable acceptance
- **Procurement Documentation**: Complete record keeping

## Monitoring and Control Standards

### Performance Monitoring
- **Key Performance Indicators**: Project success metrics
- **Dashboard Reporting**: Visual performance tracking
- **Milestone Tracking**: Critical milestone monitoring
- **Issue Management**: Problem identification and resolution
- **Change Control**: Scope, schedule, and budget changes

### Project Controls
- **Progress Tracking**: Work completion measurement
- **Variance Analysis**: Planned vs. actual comparison
- **Corrective Actions**: Performance improvement measures
- **Preventive Actions**: Risk mitigation implementation
- **Trend Analysis**: Performance pattern identification

## Project Closure Standards

### Closure Activities
- **Deliverable Acceptance**: Final deliverable approval
- **Contract Closure**: Vendor agreement completion
- **Resource Release**: Team member transition
- **Documentation Archive**: Project record preservation
- **Celebration and Recognition**: Team acknowledgment

### Lessons Learned
- **Knowledge Capture**: Experience documentation
- **Best Practices**: Success factor identification
- **Process Improvement**: Methodology enhancement
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Organizational learning
- **Template Updates**: Standard improvement
